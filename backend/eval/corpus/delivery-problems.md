# Delivery problems

## Late deliveries

A delivery is considered late once it passes the end of the estimated window by
two full working days. Contact support at that point and we chase the carrier.
Express deliveries that miss the promised day are refunded the delivery fee.

## Changing a delivery address

The address can be changed until the parcel is dispatched. After dispatch, use
the carrier's own redirection service through the tracking link; we cannot
redirect on your behalf once the parcel has left us.

## Failed delivery attempts

Carriers attempt delivery twice. After a second failure the parcel is held at a
local depot for seven days, then returned to us. A returned parcel is refunded
minus the original delivery charge.

## Delivery to a business address

Business addresses need a company name on the address line or the carrier may
refuse the delivery. Deliveries to business addresses are not made at weekends.

## Signature requirements

Orders over £150 require a signature. A safe place cannot be nominated for these,
because the signature is what evidences delivery in a dispute.
