# Pricing and discounts

## Discount codes

One discount code applies per order. Codes cannot be combined, and a code cannot
be added after the order is placed. If a code was forgotten, cancel the order
before dispatch and reorder.

## Student and trade discounts

Students receive 10% with a verified student email address. Trade customers
receive tiered pricing based on the previous twelve months of spend, reviewed
each quarter.

## Price matching

We match the price of any UK retailer holding stock of the identical item. Send a
link before ordering. We do not match marketplace sellers, auction listings, or
prices that include a bundled item.

## Price drops after purchase

If an item drops in price within fourteen days of your order, contact support and
we refund the difference. This does not apply to items bought with a discount
code or during a sitewide sale.

## VAT on displayed prices

Displayed prices include VAT for consumer accounts and exclude VAT for verified
business accounts. The basket shows both figures before checkout.
