# Billing and invoices

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. We do not accept bank
transfer for orders below £500.

## VAT and invoices

A VAT invoice is generated automatically for every order and is available from
the order page. Business customers can add a VAT number to their account, which
then appears on all future invoices.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, the order is held for 48 hours and we retry once. After that
the order is cancelled and any reserved stock is released.

## Subscription billing

Subscriptions renew on the same day each month. Changing plan mid-cycle is
prorated: you are charged the difference immediately and the next full charge
comes on the usual renewal date.
