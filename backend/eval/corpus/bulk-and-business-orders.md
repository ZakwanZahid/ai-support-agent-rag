# Bulk and business orders

## Minimum order for trade pricing

Trade pricing starts at twenty units of a single item, or a mixed basket over
£1,000. Below that, standard consumer pricing applies even on a business account.

## Purchase orders

Verified business accounts can pay by purchase order with thirty-day terms.
Approval takes two working days and needs a company registration number.

## Bulk delivery

Orders over twenty units ship on a pallet rather than by parcel carrier. Pallet
deliveries need a contact number and a kerbside drop point; the driver will not
carry goods inside.

## Quotes

Written quotes are valid for thirty days. A quote holds the price but not the
stock, so a long-running quote may need requoting if availability changes.

## Invoicing for business accounts

Business invoices are issued at dispatch and emailed to the account's billing
contact, which can differ from the ordering user.
