# Data and privacy

## What we store

We store your account details, order history, and delivery addresses. Payment
card numbers are never stored on our systems; the payment provider holds them and
returns only a token.

## Deleting your account

Account deletion removes your profile and marketing preferences. Order records
are kept for six years because tax law requires it, but they are detached from
your profile and are no longer searchable by name.

## Marketing email

Marketing email is opt-in and can be turned off from account settings or from the
unsubscribe link. Transactional email about an order you placed is not marketing
and cannot be switched off.

## Data exports

You can request a copy of your data from account settings. The export arrives as
a download link within thirty days, usually much sooner, and the link expires
after seven days.

## Cookies

Essential cookies keep you signed in and cannot be disabled. Analytics cookies
are optional and are off until accepted.
