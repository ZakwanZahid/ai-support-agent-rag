# Product care and maintenance

## Cleaning

Wipe with a slightly damp, lint-free cloth. Do not use alcohol wipes, household
cleaners, or compressed air, all of which damage the coating and can invalidate a
warranty claim.

## Battery care

Store batteries between 20% and 80% charge if the device will be unused for more
than a month. Leaving a battery fully discharged for long periods permanently
reduces its capacity.

## Firmware updates

Firmware updates install from the companion app. Do not disconnect during an
update; an interrupted update can leave the device needing a service centre
recovery, which is not covered as a manufacturing defect.

## Storage and transport

Use the original case for transport where possible. Loose transport in a bag is
the most common cause of the cosmetic damage that the warranty excludes.

## Water resistance

Water resistance is rated for splashes only. It is not a swimming rating, and the
rating degrades with age and with every drop the device takes.
