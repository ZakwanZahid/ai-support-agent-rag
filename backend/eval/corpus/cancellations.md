# Cancelling an order

## Before dispatch

An order can be cancelled free of charge at any point before it is dispatched.
Use the cancel button on the order page. If the button is missing, the parcel has
already been packed and cancellation is no longer possible from the account.

## After dispatch

Once an order has been dispatched it cannot be cancelled. Refuse the delivery or
accept it and start a return instead. A refused delivery takes longer to refund
than a return, because the carrier holds the parcel for up to fourteen days
before it reaches our warehouse.

## Cancelling part of an order

Individual lines cannot be cancelled separately. Cancel the whole order and place
a new one with the items you still want. Stock is not reserved while you do this,
so a popular item may sell out in between.

## Cancelling a subscription

A subscription can be cancelled at any time and continues until the end of the
period already paid for. We do not refund the remainder of a period. Cancelling
does not delete your account or your order history.

## Cancellation confirmations

Every cancellation sends a confirmation email within a few minutes. If no email
arrives, the cancellation did not complete — check the order status rather than
assuming it worked.
