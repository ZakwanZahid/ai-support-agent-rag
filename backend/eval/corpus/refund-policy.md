# Refund policy

## Standard refund window

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of delivery, provided the item
is unused and in its original packaging. Refund requests are reviewed within two
business days and, once approved, the money is returned to the original payment
method.

## How long the money takes to arrive

Approved refunds are issued immediately on our side. Card issuers typically take
a further five to ten business days to show the credit on a statement. We cannot
speed this up; the delay is with the bank, not with us.

## Items that cannot be refunded

Perishable goods, personalised items, and gift cards are not eligible for a
refund. Software licences cannot be refunded once the activation key has been
revealed.

## Partial refunds

If an item is returned used, damaged by the customer, or missing parts, we may
issue a partial refund covering the resale value of the item rather than the full
purchase price.
