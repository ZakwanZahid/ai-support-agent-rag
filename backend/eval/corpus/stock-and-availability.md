# Stock and availability

## Out of stock items

An out of stock item shows an expected restock date where we have one from the
supplier. Dates are estimates and move; we do not hold your order waiting for
stock unless you ask.

## Back in stock alerts

Sign up on the product page to be emailed when an item returns. Alerts are sent
in the order they were requested, and signing up does not reserve stock.

## Pre-orders

Pre-ordered items are charged at dispatch, not at the time of ordering. If a
pre-order slips more than thirty days past the advertised date, we contact you
and you may cancel without penalty.

## Discontinued products

A discontinued product is removed from the catalogue but warranty and support
continue for the full warranty period. Spare parts are held for two years after
discontinuation.

## Stock shown at checkout

The basket reserves stock for fifteen minutes. After that the item returns to
general availability and the basket may fail at payment if it has sold out.
