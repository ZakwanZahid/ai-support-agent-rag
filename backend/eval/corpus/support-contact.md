# Contacting support

## Email

Email support is answered within one working day. Include your order number in
the subject line; messages without one are routed to a slower general queue.

## Live chat

Live chat is available Monday to Friday. Chat handles order status, delivery
chasing, and returns labels. It cannot process refunds above £200, which need an
email so there is a written record.

## Phone

Phone support is available for business accounts and for warranty claims on
hardware. Consumer order questions are handled by email and chat only.

## Escalating a complaint

Ask for an escalation in your reply and a supervisor picks it up within two
working days. Complaints unresolved after eight weeks can be taken to the
independent ombudsman, whose details are sent with the final response.

## Response times during sales

Response times roughly double during sale periods and in the week after a public
holiday. We publish current wait times at the top of the help centre.
