# Damaged and missing items

## Reporting damage

Report damage within 48 hours of delivery. Photographs of the item and of the
outer packaging are both needed: carriers dispute claims without evidence that
the box itself was damaged.

## Missing items from a parcel

If a parcel arrives with items missing, check whether the order was split across
several parcels. Split shipments are common when one item is out of stock at the
nearest warehouse, and each parcel has its own tracking number.

## Wrong item received

Keep the wrong item and contact support. We send the correct item immediately and
provide a prepaid label for the wrong one. You are not charged twice; the second
order is created at zero value.

## Parcel marked delivered but not received

Check with neighbours and look for a safe-place photograph in the tracking
details. If neither turns up the parcel, we open a carrier investigation, which
takes up to seven working days before a replacement is authorised.
