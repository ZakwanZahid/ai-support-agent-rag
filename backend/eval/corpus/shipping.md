# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery times

Standard delivery arrives in three to five business days. Express delivery
arrives the next business day when ordered before 2pm. Orders placed at the
weekend are dispatched on the following Monday.

## Shipping costs

Standard delivery is free on orders over £40. Below that threshold it costs
£3.95. Express delivery is £7.95 regardless of order value.

## Where we ship

We ship to the United Kingdom and to all EU member states. We do not currently
ship to the United States, Canada, or Australia.

## Tracking an order

A tracking link is emailed when the parcel leaves our warehouse. If the tracking
has not updated for three days, contact support and we will open an investigation
with the carrier.
