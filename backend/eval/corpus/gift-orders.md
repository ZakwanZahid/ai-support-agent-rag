# Gift orders and gift cards

## Sending an order as a gift

Tick "this is a gift" at checkout to omit prices from the packing slip and add a
short message. Gift orders ship to any address, which does not have to match the
billing address.

## Gift receipts

A gift receipt lets the recipient return an item without seeing the price. The
refund goes to the original purchaser's payment method, not to the recipient, so
agree between yourselves before returning.

## Buying gift cards

Gift cards are delivered by email within fifteen minutes and are valid for
eighteen months. They cannot be used to buy other gift cards.

## Lost gift cards

A gift card code can be resent to the original purchase email. We cannot reissue
a card whose balance has already been spent, even if it was spent by someone
else, because the code is the only proof of ownership.

## Gift card balances

Any unspent balance stays on the card for use on a later order. A card cannot be
exchanged for cash.
