# Warranty

## What is covered

All hardware carries a two-year warranty covering manufacturing defects. The
warranty covers parts and labour, and we pay the shipping both ways for a valid
warranty claim.

## What is not covered

Accidental damage, liquid damage, and normal wear such as scratched casing are
not covered. Damage caused by third-party repairs voids the remaining warranty.

## Making a claim

Warranty claims need the order number and a description of the fault. Photographs
help. Most claims are assessed within three working days.

## Replacement or repair

We decide whether to repair or replace based on the fault. If a replacement is
not available, we offer a refund at the original purchase price.
